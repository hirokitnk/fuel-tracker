How it's calculated

Base burn = basal metabolic rate (Mifflin-St Jeor) × activity factor (office or home), rounded to the nearest 100 kcal. A base burn entered on the 設定 tab takes priority. Exercise extra: run = Strava × 0.93, swim = minutes swimming × 8.5 kcal/min (from METs), Zwift = × 0.8, bike = × 0.6, strength = × 0.7, anything else = × 0.7. Intake target = burn − deficit target. Energy availability (intake − exercise extra) has a floor of fat-free mass × a factor. Protein and carbs are per kg of body weight; fat is a ceiling. Carbs use the higher target on days with ≥ 400 kcal of exercise extra. Nuts are estimated about 15% below food-table values to account for absorption.
